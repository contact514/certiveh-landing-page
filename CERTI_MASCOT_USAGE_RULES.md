# Certi — Mascot Usage Rulebook
## CertiVeh Brand Guidelines Appendix v1.0 — June 2026

**Scope**: This document governs ALL usage of "Certi," the cartoon caiman mascot, across marketing and communications channels. Certi does NOT appear on certiveh.co (landing page) or portal.certiveh.co (user portal). The product stays clean and professional; the mascot lives exclusively in marketing.

**Audience for this document**: Designers, social media managers, copywriters, agencies, and anyone producing CertiVeh marketing materials.

---

## 0. CARDINAL RULES (non-negotiable)

1. Certi NEVER appears on the landing page (certiveh.co) or the user portal (portal.certiveh.co).
2. Certi NEVER replaces the CertiVeh logo (emerald shield + lightning bolt). They are separate assets with separate jobs.
3. Certi NEVER appears without the CertiVeh logo somewhere in the same piece (exception: WhatsApp stickers).
4. Certi is a supporting character, not the protagonist. The message always comes first.

---

## 1. FREQUENCY RULES

### 1.1 Social Media Feed (Instagram, Facebook, LinkedIn)

| Rule | Limit |
|------|-------|
| Maximum Certi posts per rolling 5-post window | **2 out of 5** |
| Minimum gap between Certi posts | **1 non-Certi post** between them |
| Maximum consecutive Certi posts | **Never** — always alternate |

**In practice**: If you publish 10 posts per week, a maximum of 4 may feature Certi. The other 6 must be Certi-free (infographics, vehicle photos, client results, brand-only content).

### 1.2 Carousels

| Carousel length | Max slides with Certi |
|-----------------|----------------------|
| 3-5 slides | **1 slide** (intro or closing only) |
| 6-8 slides | **2 slides** (intro AND closing, never consecutive middle slides) |
| 9-10 slides | **2 slides** (same rule as above) |

Certi must NEVER appear on more than 2 slides in any single carousel, regardless of length.

### 1.3 Email Sequences

| Sequence length | Max emails with Certi |
|-----------------|----------------------|
| 1 email (standalone) | **1** (allowed, not required) |
| 2-3 email sequence | **1 email** |
| 4-6 email sequence | **2 emails** (never consecutive) |
| 7+ email sequence | **Max 30%** of emails, never consecutive |

Certi should appear in the FIRST or a MID-FUNNEL email, NEVER in the final conversion/CTA-heavy email. The closing email must be brand-only.

### 1.4 Within a Single Piece (post, story, flyer, email)

| Format | Max Certi appearances |
|--------|----------------------|
| Single-image post | **1 instance** of Certi |
| Story frame (single) | **1 instance** of Certi |
| Flyer / one-pager | **1 instance** of Certi |
| Email body | **1 instance** (header OR inline, not both) |
| Video thumbnail | **1 instance** of Certi |

Certi must NEVER be duplicated, tiled, or patterned within a single composition.

## 2. SIZE AND HIERARCHY RULES

### 2.1 Maximum Size Relative to Canvas

| Context | Max Certi size (% of canvas area) | Max Certi height (% of canvas height) |
|---------|-----------------------------------|----------------------------------------|
| Social media post (1080x1080) | **15% of total area** | **40% of canvas height** |
| Story (1080x1920) | **12% of total area** | **30% of canvas height** |
| Email header (600px wide) | **10% of total area** | **25% of header height** |
| Print flyer (A4/Letter) | **10% of total area** | **30% of page height** |
| Video frame | **12% of total area** | **30% of frame height** |

**Rule of thumb**: If Certi is taller than the headline text block, Certi is too big. Reduce.

### 2.2 Minimum Size

Certi must NEVER be rendered smaller than **80px tall** in digital or **15mm tall** in print. Below this threshold, details are lost and the mascot looks like a smudge.

### 2.3 Visual Hierarchy (ranked 1 = most prominent)

| Priority | Element | Notes |
|----------|---------|-------|
| **1** | Headline / Key message | Always the largest, most prominent element |
| **2** | CTA (button, link, action) | Must be immediately visible |
| **3** | CertiVeh logo | Always present; placed in header, footer, or corner |
| **4** | Certi mascot | Supporting role — draws attention toward #1 and #2, never away from them |
| **5** | Body copy / details | Secondary information |

### 2.4 Logo Co-presence Rule

Every piece that includes Certi MUST also include the CertiVeh logo (shield + bolt + wordmark or shield + bolt icon-only). Placement options:

- Top-left or top-center (preferred)
- Bottom-right corner lockup
- Watermark position (for video)

**Exception**: WhatsApp sticker packs — Certi may appear solo since the stickers are used in branded conversation contexts where the logo is impractical.

## 3. CONTEXT RULES

### 3.1 Topics/Themes WHERE Certi IS Allowed

| Topic | Example use |
|-------|-------------|
| Educational content (how-to, explainers) | Certi pointing at a step-by-step graphic |
| Tax savings tips | Certi celebrating next to a savings number |
| Process reminders (document deadlines, UPME windows) | Certi with a clock or calendar prop |
| New vehicle model announcements (eligible models) | Certi waving next to the car |
| Seasonal greetings (holidays, year-end) | Certi in themed outfit or pose |
| Community engagement (polls, Q&A, quizzes) | Certi asking a question |
| Welcome messages (new followers, onboarding) | Certi waving hello |
| Milestone celebrations (X certificates issued) | Certi celebrating |
| Memes and humor (light, brand-safe) | Certi in relatable EV-owner situations |
| WhatsApp notifications (non-transactional) | Certi sticker greeting |

### 3.2 Topics/Themes WHERE Certi IS FORBIDDEN

| Topic | Why | What to use instead |
|-------|-----|---------------------|
| Legal disclaimers and terms of service | Undermines legal seriousness | Brand-only, no illustration |
| Error messages or service outages | Mascot trivializes user frustration | CertiVeh logo + empathetic copy |
| Pricing and payment pages/posts | Distracts from financial decision | Clean brand layout |
| Complaints or crisis communication | Mascot looks tone-deaf | Official brand voice, logo only |
| Government/UPME official correspondence | Must appear institutional | CertiVeh logo + formal layout |
| Testimonials and case studies | Real people should be the focus | Photo of person + brand frame |
| Data privacy and security messaging | Requires institutional trust | Shield icon + formal design |
| Partner/B2B co-branded materials | Looks unprofessional to partners | CertiVeh logo lockup only |
| Job postings and HR communications | Inappropriate casualness | Brand-only, professional layout |
| Paid ads focused on conversion (bottom-funnel) | Certi reduces conversion signal clarity | Logo + CTA + benefit headline |

### 3.3 Brand Voice Without Certi

When Certi is absent, CertiVeh speaks as the institutional brand: confident, clear, empowering, and Colombian. Use the emerald shield identity, Inter typography, and the established tone from the Brand Manual. This is the DEFAULT voice. Certi is the exception, not the rule.

## 4. VISUAL RULES

### 4.1 Background Requirements

| Background type | Allowed? | Notes |
|-----------------|----------|-------|
| White (#FFFFFF) | YES | Default, preferred |
| Slate-50 (#F8FAFC) | YES | Light gray, clean |
| Emerald-50 (#ECFDF5) | YES | Light green, on-brand |
| Teal-50 (#F0FDFA) | YES | Light teal, on-brand |
| Slate-900 (#0F172A) | YES | Dark mode — use Certi's light variant |
| Brand gradient (emerald-to-teal) | YES | Use Certi's light variant |
| Photography with overlay (70%+ opacity dark) | YES | Use Certi's light variant |
| Busy photography without overlay | NO | Certi gets lost in visual noise |
| Bright/neon solid colors | NO | Off-brand |
| Non-brand gradients | NO | Off-brand |
| Patterned or textured backgrounds | NO | Competes with Certi's detail |

### 4.2 Clear Space

Maintain a clear space around Certi equal to **1x Certi's head width** on all sides. No text, logos, borders, or other graphic elements may enter this zone.

```
Example: If Certi's head is 60px wide, maintain 60px of clear space
on top, bottom, left, and right of the full Certi illustration.
```

### 4.3 Cropping Rules

| Modification | Allowed? | Conditions |
|--------------|----------|------------|
| Crop at waist (half-body) | YES | Only from below; head and torso must be fully visible |
| Crop at chest (peek-in from edge) | YES | Used for corner placements; min 60% of Certi visible |
| Crop head or face | NEVER | The face is the identity — it must always be fully visible |
| Crop tail only | YES | Tail can exit the canvas edge |

### 4.4 Transformation Rules

| Modification | Allowed? |
|--------------|----------|
| Horizontal flip (mirror) | YES — only to match layout flow (e.g., Certi looks toward the CTA) |
| Vertical flip | NEVER |
| Rotation up to 15 degrees | YES — for playful angles in Stories/Reels only |
| Rotation beyond 15 degrees | NEVER |
| Scaling (proportional) | YES — within min/max size rules |
| Stretching or skewing | NEVER |
| Recoloring to brand palette variants | SEE BELOW |
| Recoloring to off-brand colors | NEVER |
| Adding drop shadows | YES — soft, subtle only (max 20% opacity, 4-8px blur) |
| Adding glows or outlines | NEVER |
| 3D effects or bevels | NEVER |
| Applying filters (blur, grain, duotone) | NEVER |
| Overlaying text on top of Certi | NEVER — text goes beside or above Certi, never overlapping |

### 4.5 Recoloring Rules

Certi's canonical colors must be used in 95% of cases. Permitted recolor exceptions:

| Variant | When allowed |
|---------|-------------|
| Monochrome emerald-600 silhouette | Watermarks, subtle background patterns |
| Monochrome white silhouette | On dark backgrounds, as a secondary element only |
| Monochrome slate-900 silhouette | On light backgrounds, as a secondary element only |

Full-color Certi is ALWAYS preferred. Monochrome variants are only for decorative/secondary use, never as the primary Certi instance in a composition.

### 4.6 Dark vs. Light Background Treatment

| Background | Certi version | Logo version |
|------------|---------------|--------------|
| White / light gray / light green | Full-color Certi (default) | Logo `variant="default"` |
| Slate-900 / black / dark overlay | Full-color Certi with light outline stroke (2px white) | Logo `variant="light"` |
| Brand gradient | Full-color Certi with light outline stroke (2px white) | Logo `variant="light"` |

On dark backgrounds, Certi must have a subtle white outline or be placed on a light "badge" shape (circle or rounded rectangle in white or emerald-50) to maintain visibility and separation.

## 5. COPY RULES

### 5.1 First-Person Voice

Certi may speak in first person ONLY in these formats:

| Format | Allowed? | Example |
|--------|----------|---------|
| Speech bubble in illustration | YES | "Yo te explico paso a paso" |
| Instagram Story text overlay | YES | "Hoy te cuento como ahorrar en tu declaracion" |
| WhatsApp sticker text | YES | "Listo, parcero!" |
| Social media caption (full post) | NO | The caption must be written as CertiVeh, not as Certi |
| Email body text | NO | The email sender is CertiVeh, not Certi |
| Ad headline | NO | Brand voice only |
| Blog articles | NO | Institutional voice |

**Key rule**: Certi may "speak" inside visual elements (bubbles, callouts) but NEVER in the main body copy of any channel. The body copy is always CertiVeh's institutional voice.

### 5.2 Referencing Certi by Name

| Context | Allowed? | Example |
|---------|----------|---------|
| Social media caption introducing Certi | YES | "Certi te trae los 3 datos que necesitas saber..." |
| Third-person reference in copy | YES | "Como dice Certi: sin certificado, no hay beneficio" |
| Email subject line | YES, max 1 per sequence | "Certi te recuerda: cierra la ventana UPME el viernes" |
| Paid ad headline | NO | Use CertiVeh brand name only |
| Legal or official documents | NEVER | Only "CertiVeh" or "CertiVeh S.A.S." |

### 5.3 Tone Shift: Certi Present vs. Absent

| Attribute | Certi PRESENT | Certi ABSENT |
|-----------|---------------|--------------|
| Formality | Casual-friendly (tuteo, colloquial) | Professional-warm (tuteo, but structured) |
| Humor | Allowed — light, relatable | Minimal — only if natural |
| Colombianisms | Encouraged ("parcero," "bacano," "pilas") | Selective — one per piece max |
| Emojis in copy | 2-3 per post max | 0-1 per post max |
| Exclamation marks | Allowed freely | Max 1 per piece |
| Technical language | Simplified, Certi "translates" jargon | Clear but can include proper terms |
| CTA tone | Playful ("Dale, calcula tu ahorro") | Direct ("Calcular mi beneficio") |

## 6. CHANNEL-SPECIFIC RULES

### 6.1 Instagram Feed Posts (1080x1080 or 1080x1350)

- **Certi position**: Bottom-right corner or bottom-left corner (never centered, never top)
- **Max Certi height**: 40% of canvas height
- **Logo**: Top-left corner or top-center, always
- **Certi frequency**: Max 2 out of every 5 posts
- **Caption**: CertiVeh voice; may reference Certi by name ("Certi te explica...")
- **Certi variant**: Use the variant that matches the post emotion (see Section 7)

### 6.2 Instagram Stories (1080x1920)

- **Certi position**: Bottom third of the frame (below the fold of the message)
- **Max Certi height**: 30% of canvas height
- **Logo**: Top-left, small (icon-only variant acceptable at 48px)
- **Certi frequency**: Max 3 out of every 7 story frames in a single story sequence
- **Interactive elements**: Certi may point toward polls, quizzes, or swipe-up CTAs
- **Rotation**: Up to 15 degrees allowed for playful feel
- **Speech bubbles**: Allowed in Stories; keep text to 8 words or fewer

### 6.3 TikTok / Reels (1080x1920 video)

- **Certi as overlay**: Lower-left or lower-right corner, static or with subtle bounce animation
- **Max Certi height**: 25% of frame height (smaller due to motion context)
- **Certi on-screen duration**: Certi may appear for the first 3 seconds (hook) and last 2 seconds (CTA), NOT throughout the entire video
- **Certi as main character in animation**: Allowed for dedicated Certi explainer series only; max 1 per week
- **Logo**: Persistent watermark, top-left, icon-only, 32-40px, 70% opacity
- **Text-to-speech voice for Certi**: NOT allowed. Use CertiVeh's narration voice. Certi is visual only in video.

### 6.4 WhatsApp

- **Sticker packs**: Certi may appear WITHOUT the CertiVeh logo (only exception to Rule 0.3)
- **Sticker count per pack**: 8-12 stickers; each must be a DIFFERENT Certi variant/pose
- **Broadcast messages**: Certi may appear as a small inline image (max 200px wide) at the TOP of the message, once
- **Notification templates**: Certi is FORBIDDEN in transactional messages (payment confirmations, certificate status updates). Allowed only in marketing broadcasts.
- **Profile picture**: Use CertiVeh shield icon, NOT Certi. The mascot is not the brand avatar.

### 6.5 Email

- **Certi placement**: Header illustration OR mid-body callout, NEVER both in the same email
- **Max Certi width**: 200px in a 600px email layout
- **Alt text**: Always include `alt="Certi, la mascota de CertiVeh"` for accessibility
- **Email signature**: Certi is FORBIDDEN in the email signature block. Use CertiVeh logo only.
- **Subject line**: May reference Certi by name (max 1 email per sequence, see Section 1.3)
- **Transactional emails** (receipts, confirmations, status updates): Certi is FORBIDDEN. Brand-only.
- **Marketing emails** (newsletters, promotions, educational): Certi is ALLOWED per frequency rules.

### 6.6 Print Materials (flyers, brochures, event banners)

- **Certi position**: Bottom-right or lower section; never above the headline
- **Max Certi height**: 30% of the printed piece height
- **Logo**: Must be at LEAST 2x the height of Certi in the same piece (logo dominance)
- **Bleed**: Certi may bleed off the bottom or side edge but NEVER off the top edge
- **Print resolution**: Certi assets must be 300dpi minimum; never upscale from screen assets
- **Event banners / roll-ups**: Certi at the base of the banner only; max 25% of banner height

### 6.7 Paid Ads (Meta, Google Display, YouTube Pre-roll)

- **Top-of-funnel (awareness)**: Certi ALLOWED — educational, playful variants
- **Mid-funnel (consideration)**: Certi ALLOWED but must be smaller than the CTA button area
- **Bottom-funnel (conversion)**: Certi FORBIDDEN — clean, brand-only creative with logo + CTA + benefit
- **Google Display**: Certi max 20% of ad canvas area (to keep ads clean at small sizes)
- **Video pre-roll ads**: Certi may appear in first 2 seconds only, then transition to brand content
- **A/B testing rule**: Always test a Certi variant against a no-Certi variant. Do not assume Certi improves performance.

## 7. VARIANT SELECTION GUIDE

### 7.1 Quick Reference Table

| Variant Name | Pose / Expression | Use When... | Forbidden When... |
|-------------|-------------------|-------------|-------------------|
| **Certi Saluda** | Waving, smiling | Welcomes, introductions, new follower greetings, first email in sequence | Serious topics, complaints |
| **Certi Explica** | Pointing up, one finger raised, confident | How-to content, educational carousels, process explainers, FAQ posts | Celebrations, urgency |
| **Certi Celebra** | Arms up, confetti, huge grin | Milestones, certificate issued, savings revealed, seasonal greetings | Error states, legal content, pricing |
| **Certi Pilas** | Pointing at viewer, alert expression | Deadline reminders, UPME window alerts, "don't miss out" content | Casual greetings, celebrations |
| **Certi Relax** | Leaning back, sunglasses, thumbs up | "We handle everything" messaging, stress-free process, automation benefits | Urgency, deadlines, errors |
| **Certi Pregunta** | Head tilted, question mark, curious | Polls, quizzes, Q&A stories, engagement posts | Announcements, CTAs |
| **Certi Ahorro** | Holding coins/money bag, winking | Tax savings content, calculator results, financial benefit posts | Non-financial topics, formal content |
| **Certi Thumbs Up** | Simple thumbs up, neutral smile | Generic approvals, confirmations, "good to know" tips | Strong emotions needed (celebration, urgency) |
| **Certi Documento** | Holding a certificate/paper, proud | Certificate-related content, document tips, UPME process content | Non-process topics |
| **Certi Eco** | With a leaf or EV charging cable | Sustainability content, EV lifestyle posts, environmental benefits | Financial/tax content, urgency |


## 8. CHECKLIST FOR EVERY CERTI PIECE

Before publishing any piece that includes Certi, verify ALL of the following:

- [ ] This piece is for a marketing/comms channel, NOT the landing page or portal
- [ ] The topic is on the ALLOWED list (Section 3.1), not the FORBIDDEN list (Section 3.2)
- [ ] Frequency limits have not been exceeded (Section 1)
- [ ] Certi appears only ONCE in this piece (Section 1.4)
- [ ] Certi is smaller than the headline and CTA (Section 2.3 hierarchy)
- [ ] Certi does not exceed max size for this format (Section 2.1)
- [ ] Certi is not smaller than 80px tall / 15mm (Section 2.2)
- [ ] The CertiVeh logo is present in the piece (Section 2.4)
- [ ] Clear space around Certi equals 1x head width (Section 4.2)
- [ ] Certi's face is fully visible, not cropped (Section 4.3)
- [ ] No text overlaps Certi (Section 4.4)
- [ ] Background is from the approved list (Section 4.1)
- [ ] If dark background: Certi has outline stroke or badge (Section 4.6)
- [ ] Certi only "speaks" in visual elements, not body copy (Section 5.1)
- [ ] Tone matches Certi-present guidelines (Section 5.3)
- [ ] Channel-specific rules for this platform are followed (Section 6)
- [ ] The correct Certi variant is used for this emotion/context (Section 7)

## 9. VIOLATIONS AND ESCALATION

If a team member is unsure whether Certi is appropriate for a specific piece:

1. Default to NO Certi. The brand works perfectly without the mascot.
2. Consult this document's Section 3 (Context Rules) and Section 7 (Variant Selection).
3. If still unclear, escalate to the brand lead at brand@certiveh.co before publishing.

Common violations to watch for:
- Using Certi in transactional emails or portal UI
- Exceeding frequency limits because "it looks fun"
- Making Certi larger than the headline
- Using Certi in bottom-funnel conversion ads
- Placing text on top of Certi
- Using Certi without the CertiVeh logo
- Using Certi for crisis communication or complaint responses
